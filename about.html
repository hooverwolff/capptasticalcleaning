Family-run since 2016

We treat every car like it's about to be ours for the day.

Capptastical Cleaning started as a single bay and a hose. Ten years on, we're still doing the same thing that got us here: a careful, hand-finished wash, done properly, every time.

What we stand for

Three things guide every wash we do.

01 — Care

Hand-finished, not rushed

No automated tunnels. Every car gets a real person paying attention to it, start to finish.

02 — Honesty

Prices you see up front

What you're quoted online is what you pay. No surprise add-ons at the till.

03 — Water-wise

Low-waste by design

Reclaimed water systems and biodegradable products, because it's the local creek we're rinsing towards.

How we got here

2016

One bay, one hose

Started washing cars on weekends out of a rented lot on Harbourview Road.

2019

First full-time crew

Brought on our first two wash technicians and opened seven days a week.

2022

Added Deluxe & Valet

Introduced interior detailing packages after one too many requests for "just the inside too".

2026

Booking made easy

Launched online booking so you can skip the phone call and just pick a time.
